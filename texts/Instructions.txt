How to use Webscraper:

Step 1: Downloading and installing Ollama

You need to download a program called Ollama to get acess to different AI Models.
Go to: https://ollama.com/download and download the right version for your software.
Run the installation.

Step 2: Download AI Models

Start Ollama and pick the model you want, write a prompt to ollama using the model.
Ollama will answer your prompt, and download the model. This will take a while.

Step 3: Start app

Start the Webscraper app! Now you can start scraping.

Step 4: Scrape page

Go to the ScraperPage and choose a page to scrape.
A text should pop up when the scraping is finished.

Step 5: Classification

Go to the classification page. Pick the page you just scraped in the dropdown.
Start the classification, this can take a while.
A textbox will pop up when the classification is finished.

Step 6: Result and Analysis

Results:
To read your classification results go to the ResultPage and pick your csv-file.
Open the file to read more, click on the summary to read the full summary.

Analysis:
To see your classification results in a pie or bar chart, choose your csv-file
and click on "show pie chart" to see pie chart or "show bar chart" to see bar chart.
